To start, choose four level 1 spells from the Cleric spell list. __FT_UUID_00007__{Bless}, __FT_UUID_00008__{Cure Wounds}, __FT_UUID_00009__{Guiding Bolt}, and __FT_UUID_00010__{Shield of Faith} are recommended.__FT_HTML_00018____FT_HTML_00019__The number of spells on your list increases as you gain Cleric levels, as shown in the Prepared Spells column of the Cleric Features table.
